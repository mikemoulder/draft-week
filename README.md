# FTL: Fantasy Trading League

Fantasy football for trading: pick five real Strike Finance traders, and their real trades on Strike score your points.

**Live:** https://strike-ftl.vercel.app

> We didn't want to build another generic app. We wanted something innovative, something different: a new way to
> enjoy trading. So we turned the people trading on Strike into cards you pick, back and cheer on, against your
> friends, every week. That is why we built FTL.

Built during the Strike Hackathon (October 21 to 23, 2026). Work in progress.
